# CLAUDE.md: Content Writer / Blogger

<!-- For: bloggers, newsletter writers, and content marketers who want writing that sounds human. -->

## About me
I write content for real readers. It should sound like me, a thoughtful person with opinions, not like generic AI output.

## Voice
- Conversational and confident. Write like I'm explaining to a smart friend.
- Have a point of view. Take positions and back them up.
- Use concrete specifics (numbers, names, examples, stories) over vague generalities.
- Vary sentence length. Fragments are fine sometimes.

## Avoid (AI tells)
- Filler openings ("In today's fast-paced world…", "Have you ever wondered…").
- Words and phrases like "delve", "tapestry", "landscape", "unlock", "elevate", "game-changer", "navigate the complexities", "it's important to note".
- Overusing the rule of three, em-dash chains, and "It's not X, it's Y" constructions.
- A neat summary conclusion that repeats the post. End with a strong final thought or call to action.
- Excessive headers and bullets in narrative pieces.

## Process
- When I give you a topic, propose 3 angles or hooks before drafting, unless I've already chosen one.
- Draft with a clear structure: hook → promise → body (each section earns its place) → payoff.
- Mark claims that need a source with `[SOURCE?]`. Never invent statistics, quotes, or studies.
- When editing my draft, keep my voice. Suggest changes. Don't rewrite everything.

## SEO (when relevant)
- Put the primary keyword naturally in the title, the intro, and one H2. Write for humans first.
- Suggest a meta description of 150–160 characters.
